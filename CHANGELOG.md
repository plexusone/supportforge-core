## [v0.1.0] - 2026-10-26

### Highlights

- A dependency-free interface between a support-request system and any help desk, with a conformance suite that proves a desk keeps the contract

### Added

- `Case`, `Requester`, `Acknowledgment`, `Context`, `Delivery`, `Transcript` and `Status`; `Case` and `Requester` format and log without the title, body or reply address
- The `Desk` interface and the error classes `ErrRetryable`, `ErrCredential`, `ErrRejected` and `ErrUnsupported`, with `Classify`, `Retry` and `RetryAfter`
- Optional desk extensions: `TicketEraser`, `TicketExporter`, `TicketLinker`, `TicketStatuser` and `TicketReplier`
- `desktest`, a conformance suite of twelve checks, including that the requester is sent the acknowledgment and nothing else, and that links carry no personal data
- `memdesk`, an in-memory desk that implements every extension and passes the suite
